The Specific Threat of Voice Cloning in 2026

The threat is becoming highly accessible. Scammers have been documented using AI tools to clone the voices, images, and written messages of U.S. political candidates leading up to the 2026 midterms, often for scams like stealing cash and harvesting voter data. This suggests a movement toward highly targeted, localized attacks.
