The focus of AI lobbying is shifting toward securing exemptions from existing regulatory frameworks. The Push for Regulatory Exemptions and Frameworks

The strategic goal of this increased lobbying appears to be guiding or halting stricter regulatory measures. While some voices advocate for comprehensive oversight, such as Rep. Ro Khanna proposing a federal agency to oversee AI akin to the FDA, the industry push seems geared toward carving out exceptions. One area where regulatory ambiguity exists is in how federal agencies handle AI use, with the National Archives stating that AI platform use by agencies does not automatically generate federal records.

  • Some lawmakers, like Rep. Ro Khanna, propose creating a dedicated AI oversight agency.
  • Guidance from the National Archives suggests AI use in agencies does not automatically create federal records.

Conclusion

The evidence strongly indicates that AI firms are proactively lobbying to secure specific carve-outs from regulation, rather than merely influencing the general direction of policy. The documented increases in lobbying spending by major players, coupled with the reported industry push to halt tougher regulation, points to a tactical effort to achieve exemptions. While there is a proposal for a comprehensive regulatory body, the current documented activity suggests a more granular fight over regulatory boundaries.
