FIELD NOTE

The Rise of AI Trading Bot Activity

AI trading bots have become increasingly prevalent across crypto exchanges, with multiple platforms now offering integrated bot functionality. According to industry guides, these bots employ various strategies including grid trading, dollar-cost averaging, arbitrage, and signal-based trading.

The growth in bot activity is driven by their ability to execute trades faster and more consistently than human traders. Platforms like 3Commas, Pionex, and Cryptohopper have become popular among algorithmic traders, while exchanges themselves are incorporating bot features directly into their interfaces.

  • Exchanges integrating native bot functionality
