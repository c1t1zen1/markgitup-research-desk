Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is increasingly scrutinizing the use of Artificial Intelligence within financial institutions, particularly hedge funds. New draft guidance is reportedly requiring these firms to conduct stress tests on LLM-driven strategies. This regulatory focus brings to light critical, unresolved technical risks associated with these advanced models, namely the potential for 'hallucination'—where models generate false yet convincing information.

Quantifying the Hallucination Threat
