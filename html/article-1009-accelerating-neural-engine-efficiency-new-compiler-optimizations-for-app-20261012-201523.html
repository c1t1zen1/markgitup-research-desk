FIELD NOTE

The Hardware Foundation: Apple Silicon and the Neural Engine

Apple's approach to AI is deeply rooted in its custom silicon strategy, which integrates components like the CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine onto a unified die. This architecture has been a core component of Apple's AI strategy for many years. The Neural Engine (ANE) itself functions as a fixed-function matrix accelerator present in chips dating back to the A11-class iPhone and iPad, and the M1-class Mac chips. Some analyses report on the ANE's architecture and performance based on measurements and static analysis.

  • The ANE is a fixed-function matrix accelerator within Apple systems-on-chip.
  • Apple's AI strategy emphasizes custom silicon integration rather than relying solely on large data centers.
  • Apple Silicon integrates the CPU, GPU, Neural Engine, and I/O on a unified die.
