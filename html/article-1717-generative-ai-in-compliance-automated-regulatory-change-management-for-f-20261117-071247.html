Conclusion

The evidence strongly indicates a trend where GenAI is becoming a critical tool for managing the inherent instability of global financial regulations. The current focus is shifting from simply generating required reports to using AI to *understand* and *adapt* to regulatory changes in real-time. While the sources confirm the existence of regulatory scrutiny and guidance, specific details on GenAI's proactive workflow adaptation capabilities are not detailed, suggesting this is an emerging frontier.
