Why this matters now

This article examines the emerging paradigm shift in international intelligence, moving away from purely classified data toward sophisticated analysis of publicly available information (OSINT) powered by advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI). Defining the Tools: AI and OSINT

AI is generally defined as technology enabling machines to simulate human learning, processing information, and predicting outcomes (IBM; Britannica). Concurrently, Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) has emerged as a critical component for tracking global events, providing perspectives outside of traditional media channels (InfostreamGlobal).

Conclusion
