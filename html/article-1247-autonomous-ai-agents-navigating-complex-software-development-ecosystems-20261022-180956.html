Conclusion

The evidence confirms that AI is actively reshaping software development, moving beyond simple code generation into integrated workflows spanning multiple tools. While the potential for AI to reach human-level problem-solving exists, the current evidence focuses more on the integration of AI assistants into existing development processes. The practical challenge lies in ensuring these disparate AI components operate coherently across the entire software lifecycle, a point highlighted by discussions on consistent sourcing within workflows.
