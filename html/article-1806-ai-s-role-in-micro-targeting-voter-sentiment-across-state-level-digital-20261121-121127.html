Closing read

Conclusion

In summary, the evidence points to AI's migration from a theoretical concept to a practical tool in state-level political strategy, enabling highly precise micro-targeting. The technology is capable of analyzing voter data to predict and influence sentiment across specific demographics within a state. It is important to distinguish between documented uses—such as predictive modeling in 2026 campaigns—and projections, such as the potential for AI to sway millions of voters' decisions in that same year. The transformation is evident, yet the full ethical and practical scope remains under active study.
