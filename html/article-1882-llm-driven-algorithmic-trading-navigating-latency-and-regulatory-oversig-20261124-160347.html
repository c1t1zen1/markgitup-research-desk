Closing read

Conclusion

The supplied evidence is insufficient to substantiate the headline's central claims about LLM-driven high-frequency trading, latency, or increasing regulatory oversight. It supports only general LLM background, model-comparison context, and a clear warning that several sources concern law degrees rather than language models.

The forward-looking conclusion is conditional, not factual: deploying LLMs directly into latency-sensitive financial systems may warrant rigorous performance and compliance investigation, but no supplied source demonstrates the relevant risks, benefits, rules, or outcomes. Stronger conclusions require primary regulatory documents and measured trading-system evidence.
