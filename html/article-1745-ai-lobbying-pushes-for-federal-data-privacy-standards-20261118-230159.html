AI Lobbying Pushes for Federal Data Privacy Standards

AI firms are spending record sums lobbying Washington to shape emerging federal data privacy standards, with Meta leading Big Tech spending in Q3 2026.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The artificial intelligence industry is increasingly turning to Washington to influence the development of federal data privacy standards. Recent lobbying disclosures show AI companies spending record amounts on policy advocacy, with Meta topping Big Tech firms at $5.9 million in the third quarter of 2026. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Nvidia also reported record lobbying quarters, collectively spending nearly $6 million on issues including cybersecurity and child safety. This article examines the evidence of AI industry efforts to shape national data privacy legislation based on lobbying disclosure data and industry reporting.
