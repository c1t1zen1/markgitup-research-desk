What a credible inspection evaluation would need

A rigorous evaluation would need evidence about the target defect classes, image acquisition conditions, model accuracy, inference latency, sustained throughput, thermal behavior, memory use, and failure handling. None of those measurements appears in the supplied sources.

The available Apple account and sign-in pages concern account access and security rather than engineering deployment. They provide no basis for selecting hardware, designing a local inference pipeline, or claiming production suitability.

  • Define inspection targets and acceptable false-positive and false-negative rates.
  • Measure end-to-end latency and sustained throughput under production conditions.
  • Test operator review, image capture, model failure, and recovery paths.
  • Separate verified benchmarks from forecasts or implementation hypotheses.
