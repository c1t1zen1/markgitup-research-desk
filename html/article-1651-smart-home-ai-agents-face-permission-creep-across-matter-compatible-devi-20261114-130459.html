What a rigorous article would need

A defensible article would require primary or directly relevant reporting on Matter device authorization, AI-agent capability boundaries, consent flows, account roles, revocation, audit logs, or documented incidents. None of those details appears in the supplied snippets.

Until such evidence is gathered, claims about emerging permission creep should be labeled as an investigative question or forecast, not as an established trend. The current source set supports an evidence-gap finding, not a substantive risk assessment.

  • Obtain technical documentation describing permissions and control scopes.
  • Find incident reports or research documenting unauthorized device actions.
  • Compare how access is granted, limited, monitored, and revoked across compatible devices.
