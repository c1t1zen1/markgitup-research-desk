FIELD NOTE

From Post-Production to Pre-Production

Traditionally, AI video tools have been used in post-production to automate cuts, enhance visuals, or generate subtitles. However, emerging tools now support pre-production workflows by allowing filmmakers to generate rough cuts, storyboards, or even full sequences before shooting. This shift enables creators to visualize and refine their vision earlier, potentially reducing costs and improving creative alignment. Industry guides from 2026 highlight this trend as a significant change in how AI is integrated into filmmaking.
