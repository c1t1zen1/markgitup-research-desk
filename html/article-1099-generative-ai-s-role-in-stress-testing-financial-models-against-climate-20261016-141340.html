Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence strongly supports that AI, particularly generative AI, is becoming a central, structural element in finance, actively being used to enhance risk assessment beyond traditional methods. The theoretical capacity of generative models to 'produce' new scenarios aligns perfectly with the need to simulate complex, non-linear climate impacts. However, the provided sources do not contain empirical evidence detailing how generative models are currently being used to simulate specific climate change scenarios on corporate financial disclosures.

Therefore, while the trajectory points toward this application, the current evidence confirms the *potential* and *adoption* of AI in risk management, rather than confirming the specific, advanced methodology proposed in the headline. Future research must bridge the gap between general AI adoption and specialized climate modeling.
