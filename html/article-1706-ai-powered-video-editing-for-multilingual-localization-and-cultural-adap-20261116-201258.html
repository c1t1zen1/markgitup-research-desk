Defining AI Video Localization

AI-powered video localization is defined as the use of artificial intelligence to automatically adapt video content across different languages and cultures. This process integrates several automated functions, such as automated transcription, translation, voice-over generation, subtitling, and cultural adaptation, aiming to eliminate the need for manual human effort for every language version. Some sources specify that this includes AI-assisted translation, dubbing, synthetic voice, and lip-sync capabilities.
