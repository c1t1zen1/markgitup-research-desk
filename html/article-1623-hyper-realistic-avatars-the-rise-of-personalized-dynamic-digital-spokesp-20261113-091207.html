FIELD NOTE

The Shift from Static to Dynamic Presentation

Early AI video avatars were primarily used for scalable content production, such as marketing clips or training materials, where the avatar delivered pre-rendered content without viewer response. However, the technology is advancing toward interactive capabilities. Platforms are emerging that deploy AI avatars capable of holding live, two-way conversations with users. This marks a significant transformation in the application of these digital humans.

  • Early use cases focus on scalable content production, training materials, and marketing clips.
