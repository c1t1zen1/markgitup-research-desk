Blockchain and cryptographic watermarking are emerging as vital tools to verify the origin and modification history of synthetic AI video.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The proliferation of AI-generated content necessitates robust methods for establishing trust in digital media. Digital provenance, defined as verifying who created content, when, and whether it has been modified, is becoming a critical requirement in the digital landscape. Several technical solutions, including cryptographic watermarking and blockchain ledgers, are being deployed to address the challenge of verifying authenticity in an era of indistinguishable synthetic media.
