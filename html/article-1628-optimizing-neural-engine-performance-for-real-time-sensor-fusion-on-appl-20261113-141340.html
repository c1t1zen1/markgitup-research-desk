FIELD NOTE

The Architecture of Apple's AI Acceleration

Apple Silicon incorporates dedicated hardware for artificial intelligence tasks. The Neural Engine is described as a dedicated block that has been part of Apple silicon for several years, responsible for running Apple Intelligence tasks and Core ML models. More recent architectural shifts, such as those noted in the M5 chip, suggest a move toward deeper integration of AI acceleration. Specifically, the 'Neural Fusion' design in the M5 chip places dedicated neural accelerators inside every GPU core, representing an architectural change rather than just a cosmetic update.

  • The Neural Engine is a dedicated block on Apple silicon used for running Core ML models and Apple Intelligence tasks.
  • The M5 chip features 'Neural Fusion,' integrating dedicated neural accelerators within every GPU core.
