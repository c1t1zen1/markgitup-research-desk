AI Therapist Platforms Now Require Clinical Supervision in California

California now requires human clinician oversight for AI therapy platforms, shifting responsibility from automated chatbots to licensed professionals and raising new compliance and ethical questions.

Why this matters now

Recent California legislation has moved beyond banning AI therapy advertising to mandate human clinician oversight for AI therapy platforms. Evidence points to Senate Bill 903 and related measures requiring consent, disclosure, and clinician supervision. Multiple legal and professional sources confirm that licensed clinicians must remain involved in AI-assisted therapeutic encounters. The state is framing AI as a supportive tool rather than a replacement for human clinicians, emphasizing safety, accountability, and ethical practice. New Clinical Supervision Requirement

California has introduced a new requirement that AI therapy platforms must include human clinician oversight. This means AI chatbots cannot operate independently as therapists. Instead, a licensed clinician must supervise, review, and be available to step in as needed. The regulation is intended to prevent harm and ensure accountability when AI tools are used in therapeutic settings. Professional organizations and legal sources confirm that clinicians must remain involved in AI-assisted therapeutic encounters.

Legal and Professional Interpretation
