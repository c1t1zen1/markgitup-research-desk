Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence clearly demonstrates a critical operational pivot: AI is moving from a supportive tool to an active combatant in cyber defense. The necessity for machine-speed response, coupled with the documented use of AI agents in both attacks and defenses, confirms this shift. While projections indicate significant market growth and the implementation of autonomous remediation platforms, the speed of this evolution presents immediate challenges.

It must be noted that while the deployment of autonomous defense is being actively pursued by entities like the DoD, the competitive nature of this field—where attackers are also leveraging AI agents—creates a dynamic where defensive readiness is constantly being tested. The role of external factors, such as upcoming elections, may also influence the national strategy surrounding this technological shift.
