AI Election Ads Now Face Real-Time Moderation by State-Level Platforms

State-level platforms are deploying AI-powered moderation tools to flag election ads before broad distribution, creating a confusing patchwork of enforcement across jurisdictions.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

Political campaigns are increasingly using AI-generated ads, with estimates suggesting a measurable share of mid-2026 advertising contains AI elements. These tools are being used to create, edit, and distribute ads quickly and cheaply. At the same time, states are enacting laws that regulate AI political ads, including disclosure, timing, and deepfake bans. However, enforcement remains uneven, and there is no federal standard. Evidence suggests state-level platforms are beginning to implement real-time moderation, using AI to scan ads and flag problematic content before broad distribution, but these systems vary widely in scope, capability, and jurisdictional reach. The result is a patchwork of enforcement rather than a unified national system.
