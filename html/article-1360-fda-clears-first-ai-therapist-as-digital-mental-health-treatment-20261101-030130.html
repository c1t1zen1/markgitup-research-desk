FDA Clears First AI Therapist as Digital Mental Health Treatment

The FDA's first clearance of an AI therapy platform marks a regulatory milestone, establishing a pathway for digital mental health tools to transition from wellness apps to recognized medical treatments.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has cleared the first AI-powered therapy platform as a digital mental health treatment, representing a significant regulatory milestone. This decision establishes a formal pathway for AI therapy tools to move beyond the wellness app category into recognized medical treatments. The FDA's Digital Health Advisory Committee had previously convened in November 2025 to discuss the specific regulatory considerations for generative AI-enabled digital mental health devices, including a hypothetical prescription LLM therapy chatbot for adults with major depressive disorder.

The Regulatory Milestone

The FDA's clearance of an AI therapy platform represents a first-of-its-kind regulatory decision. The agency, a federal body within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, is responsible for protecting public health by ensuring the safety, efficacy, and security of medical products. This clearance establishes that AI-powered therapy can meet the FDA's standards for medical treatments.

The FDA has provided guidance on prescription drug apps and software to reduce uncertainty for developers and support innovation. From Wellness App to Medical Treatment

The distinction between wellness chatbots and regulated medical devices has been a growing concern in the AI therapy space. In 2026, FDA enforcement, thin clinical evidence, and employer benefit pilots have been forcing a reckoning between these two categories. The FDA's clearance of an AI therapy platform provides clarity on this distinction.

Previously, the FDA had authorized over 1,300 AI-enabled medical devices across various healthcare domains, but notably, none were specifically for mental health. This gap was highlighted by the Digital Health Advisory Committee's November 2025 meeting, which specifically addressed generative AI-enabled digital mental health medical devices.

What FDA Clearance Means

FDA-approved digital health tools are software, AI systems, wearables, and apps that meet FDA safety and effectiveness standards. For mental health apps, FDA approval carries significant implications for how these tools are used, prescribed, and covered by insurance. The clearance process evaluates the safety and efficacy of the digital therapeutic.

The FDA has established industry systems to facilitate submissions, including those for digital health tools. The agency's approach to digital mental health therapeutics considers whether the tool is used with or without healthcare provider oversight, reflecting the varying levels of clinical support that may be needed.

Closing read

Conclusion

The FDA's clearance of the first AI therapy platform represents a pivotal moment for digital mental health. This regulatory milestone establishes a clear pathway for AI-powered therapy tools to transition from wellness apps to recognized medical treatments. The decision follows extensive deliberation by the FDA's Digital Health Advisory Committee, which specifically addressed the regulatory considerations for generative AI-enabled digital mental health devices.

However, the evidence base for AI therapy apps remains thin, and FDA enforcement in this area is still evolving. The clearance of one platform does not guarantee that others will follow, and the regulatory landscape for AI mental health tools is likely to continue developing. As more AI therapy platforms seek FDA clearance, the agency's approach to evaluating these tools will become clearer, potentially establishing standards for the entire category.
