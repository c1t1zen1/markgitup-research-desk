Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence supports a cautious fact pattern: financial institutions are described as planning quantum-safe protocols, crypto-agility and practical readiness steps are identified, and at least one reported pilot examines quantum-resistant infrastructure across transaction and operational domains. The material does not establish that quantum attacks are currently breaking banking transaction integrity or that the sector has adopted a common migration standard.

Projection: if controlled pilots produce credible results, they could turn post-quantum security from a theoretical concern into an operational capability. The decisive proof points will be successful testing across transaction boundaries, cross-border interoperability, operational resilience, and governance. Until those results are available, quantum readiness should be treated as an active transition program rather than a completed security upgrade.
