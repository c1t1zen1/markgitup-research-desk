Conclusion

In summary, the evidence confirms that AI is actively being used in political campaigns for highly granular voter targeting, utilizing sentiment analysis and extensive data profiling to reach narrow demographics. This represents a significant shift in campaign strategy, moving beyond traditional media to algorithm-driven precision.

While the sources detail the current deployment of these tools, they also highlight associated concerns. Forecasts suggest the 2026 midterms will be a key period for observing the scale of these AI operations.
