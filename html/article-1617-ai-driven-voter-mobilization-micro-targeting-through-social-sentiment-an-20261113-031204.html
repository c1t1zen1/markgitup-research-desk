Conclusion

The evidence confirms that AI is actively being deployed in elections, moving toward granular sentiment mapping. However, the sources present a mixed picture: while AI can be a powerful tool for campaigns, its deployment is also linked to voter backlash, as demonstrated by the data center controversy in 2026. Forecasts suggest this trend of increased technological intervention will continue to reshape the electoral landscape.
