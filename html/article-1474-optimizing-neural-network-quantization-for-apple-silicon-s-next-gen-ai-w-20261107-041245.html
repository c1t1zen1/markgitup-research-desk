Conclusion

The evidence suggests that pushing the limits of on-device AI on Apple Silicon is an engineering challenge rooted in software optimization, particularly around quantization and hardware-aware kernel development. While frameworks like MLX provide a strong foundation by optimizing for unified memory, success hinges on selecting the right quantization methodology, as different methods yield different practical results.
