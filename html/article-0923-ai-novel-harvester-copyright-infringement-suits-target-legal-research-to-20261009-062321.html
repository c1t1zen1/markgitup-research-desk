Conclusion

On the facts supplied, AI copyright litigation is prominent in the source material, but the record is not enough to substantiate a separately enumerated new wave against legal research databases. It does establish a conflict between an unlicensed-copying theory and an unsettled fair-use framework, with Thomson Reuters appeals singled out as potentially significant.
