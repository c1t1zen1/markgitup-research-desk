Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence consistently treats live AI avatar latency as a pipeline problem. Encoding, model distillation, delivery, RTC, and operational tooling all appear as relevant levers, while fidelity, battery use, bandwidth, device coverage, and interruption behavior shape the resulting experience. The sources do not provide measured values or enough detail to identify a universally superior architecture.

The most defensible forecast is that global interactive broadcasts will improve through coordinated, layer-by-layer optimization and disciplined measurement. That remains a projection. Current supplied evidence supports a test framework and a list of engineering priorities, not a performance guarantee, platform ranking, or claim that photorealism and instantaneous interaction have already been reconciled.
