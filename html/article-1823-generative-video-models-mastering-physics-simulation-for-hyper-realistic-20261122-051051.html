FIELD NOTE

The Paradigm Shift: From Pixel Prediction to World Modeling

Traditional video generation often treated the task as predicting the next frame based on preceding ones. However, the cutting edge is seeing a fundamental change: world models are replacing pixel generation by simulating physics. This transition signifies a move from simply guessing pixels to actively modeling how the world behaves.

This revolution in world modeling is cited as a key development in AI video, fundamentally altering how AI constructs video content. The integration of physics simulation is viewed as a major evolution in the field.
