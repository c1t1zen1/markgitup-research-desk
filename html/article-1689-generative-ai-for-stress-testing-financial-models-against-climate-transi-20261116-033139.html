FIELD NOTE

The Evolution of Climate Stress Testing

Conventional bank stress tests typically concentrate on short-term economic shocks within a limited timeframe. In contrast, climate stress testing requires modeling physical and transition risk scenarios over decades, which necessitates accounting for factors like policy shifts, carbon regulations, and extreme weather events. Generative AI is positioned to make this process more dynamic by enabling the simultaneous running of scenario analyses across multiple potential futures. This capability represents a significant advancement over traditional modeling approaches.

  • Conventional stress tests are limited to short-term economic shocks.
  • Climate stress testing models risks across decades, including policy shifts and extreme weather.
