FIELD NOTE

Defining Autonomous AI in Finance

The concept of an autonomous AI agent is rooted in its ability to function independently and make its own decisions, meaning it is not subject to external control. This independence is key to its role in DeFi. AI agents on blockchain are described as autonomous software systems capable of interpreting goals, selecting tools, reading data, and submitting transactions without constant human input.

These systems are positioning themselves as transformative intermediaries within decentralized ecosystems, influencing power dynamics and trust mechanisms within these environments.

  • Autonomous means independent and having the power to make its own decisions.
  • AI agents on blockchain are autonomous systems that execute transactions and manage assets.
  • These agents are theorized to reconfigure trust mechanisms in decentralized ecosystems.

The Mechanics of Autonomous DeFi Execution

DeFAI describes the use of autonomous AI agents within DeFi to handle tasks like monitoring markets, managing risk, and executing on-chain actions without continuous human involvement. These intelligent systems can manage digital assets, own wallets, and interact with decentralized protocols.

The capability of these agents lies in connecting reasoning with execution—the ability to plan and then act upon that plan within the blockchain environment. This moves beyond simple automated trading into self-directed management.
