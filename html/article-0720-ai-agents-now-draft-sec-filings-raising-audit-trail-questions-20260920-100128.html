Audit Trail Challenges

The autonomous drafting of filings by AI agents creates novel questions about audit trails and verification. Traditional audit processes assume human authorship and decision-making, but AI-generated content complicates this assumption. Regulators and auditors are now grappling with how to verify the accuracy and completeness of AI-drafted filings, particularly when it comes to understanding the decision-making process behind specific disclosures.

  • Traditional audit processes assume human authorship, which is challenged by AI drafting
  • Verifying AI-generated content requires new approaches to audit trails
  • Understanding the decision-making process behind AI disclosures is complex

The Emergence of Algorithmic Boilerplate

While AI drafting can produce error-free documents that read well, there is a risk that it could lead to the development of standardized legal language that becomes less effective for decision-making purposes. This phenomenon, known as 'algorithmic boilerplate,' could reduce the value of disclosures by making them too similar across companies, thereby reducing their usefulness to investors and other stakeholders.

  • AI drafting can produce error-free documents that read well
  • There is a risk of standardized legal language becoming less effective for decision-making
  • This could reduce the value of disclosures to investors and stakeholders
