FIELD NOTE

The Mechanics of Sentiment Analysis in Finance

Sentiment analysis involves measuring the mood surrounding a stock by processing text from various sources, including news and social media. This technique allows investors to gauge the general market mood. Tools and methods exist to measure this sentiment, though challenges remain, such as handling code-mixed text found in some social media environments. The ability to quantify this sentiment from social media posts is becoming a significant factor for traders.

  • Sentiment analysis measures stock market mood using news and social media.
  • There is an increasing demand for sentiment analysis of code-mixed social media text.
