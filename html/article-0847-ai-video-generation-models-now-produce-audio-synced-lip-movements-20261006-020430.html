Amazon Prime Video's Lip-Sync Implementation

Amazon Prime Video has become the first major streaming platform to implement AI lip-syncing technology for dubbed content. The feature debuted with the series 'Maxton Hall,' which was the first programming on the platform to feature the technology synchronizing actors' lip movements with human-dubbed audio.

The technology uses AI to adjust the lip movement of actors, making them appear natural when viewers are playing dubbed audio. This addresses a long-standing issue with dubbed content where lip movements don't match the spoken words in the target language. The feature is now available with the first two seasons of 'Maxton Hall' and will be used in Season 3, which premieres in December 2026.
