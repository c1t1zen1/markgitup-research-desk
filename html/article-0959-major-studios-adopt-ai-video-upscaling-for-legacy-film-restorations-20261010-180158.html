How AI Upscaling Works

Every modern AI upscaler begins with a convolutional or transformer-based feature extractor that decomposes each frame into multi-scale latent representations. This approach allows the AI to understand the structural content of the footage and make intelligent decisions about enhancement. Some tools offer one-pass AI enhancement that combines upscaling and noise-aware restoration without requiring frame-by-frame setup.

  • Uses convolutional or transformer-based feature extractors
