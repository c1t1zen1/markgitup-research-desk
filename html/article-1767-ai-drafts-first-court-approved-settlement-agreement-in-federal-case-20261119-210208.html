Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

On July 20, 2026, U.S. District Judge Araceli Martinez-Olguín granted final approval to a landmark $1.5 billion class-action settlement between AI firm Anthropic and a massive class of authors and publishers. This settlement, which had been preliminarily approved on September 25, 2025, represents a significant moment in the evolving landscape of intellectual property law, particularly concerning the use of copyrighted works in AI training. The settlement includes approximately $3,000 per work for roughly 482,000 works, along with the destruction of the pirated files. This case highlights the growing intersection of AI technology and legal frameworks, as courts grapple with the implications of AI-generated content and the rights of original creators.
