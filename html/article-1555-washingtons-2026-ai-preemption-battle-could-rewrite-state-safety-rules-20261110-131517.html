Conclusion

The evidence points to a critical juncture where industry actors are actively lobbying for federal preemption to standardize AI safety rules, potentially overriding the 115 state laws enacted in 2026. While the push for a single national framework is evident through lobbying surges, the political reality—including opposition from certain advocates and the current legislative control—suggests the battle over preemption remains fiercely contested.

Forecasts indicate that the outcome hinges on whether Congress can overcome the coalition dynamics that have historically defeated preemption language. If it fails, the patchwork of state laws will likely continue to define AI safety rules.
