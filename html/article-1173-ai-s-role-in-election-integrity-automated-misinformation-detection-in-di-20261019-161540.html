Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence suggests that while AI presents a significant, growing threat through sophisticated synthetic media like deepfakes, defensive measures are actively being developed across platforms, civic groups, and research institutions. The capacity of AI to correct falsehoods is under active study, indicating a dual potential for both fueling and fighting disinformation.

However, the sources also indicate structural challenges, including the need for federal capability rebuilding that may be subject to political timelines, and the current state of less regulation. Therefore, while detection is a key focus, the effectiveness of these defensive measures is contingent on policy implementation and public digital literacy.

Five takeaways

  • AI disinformation threats, including deepfakes, are increasing in sophistication and volume ahead of 2026 elections.
  • Defense against AI misinformation relies on a multi-pronged approach involving platform countermeasures and civic education.
  • Research is actively investigating the efficacy of AI chatbots in correcting election falsehoods.
  • The regulatory landscape remains challenged, with some necessary federal actions potentially awaiting political shifts.
  • AI technology is recognized as a dual-use technology, capable of both fueling and fighting election disinformation.
