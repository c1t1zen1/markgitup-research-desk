FIELD NOTE

Defining the Landscape: AI and DeFAI

Artificial intelligence is broadly defined as the ability of machines to simulate human learning and perform tasks commonly associated with humans. The concept of DeFAI represents an emerging category where autonomous AI agents operate directly within DeFi protocols. These agents are described as capable of executing swaps, managing yield strategies, rebalancing portfolios, and running risk monitoring without constant human input. This integration represents a significant shift in how decentralized finance functions.

Conclusion

The evidence strongly indicates a trajectory toward highly autonomous DeFi systems driven by AI agents, encompassing everything from automated portfolio management to self-governance. Factually, DeFAI involves AI agents executing functions within DeFi protocols, and research suggests these agents can reconfigure trust dynamics. However, the sources present these applications as emerging concepts, with projections pointing toward 'sentient protocols' and future governance models. The risk of systemic failure, exemplified by past protocol hacks, remains a critical counterpoint to the promise of autonomous management.
