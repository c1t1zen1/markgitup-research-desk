FDA Approves First AI-Driven Digital Therapeutic for Treatment-Resistant Depression

The FDA's first approval of an AI-driven digital therapeutic for treatment-resistant depression marks a regulatory milestone, signaling a shift from app-based tools to clinically validated treatments.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved the first AI-driven digital therapeutic for treatment-resistant depression, a significant regulatory milestone. This approval moves beyond traditional app-based mental health tools to a clinically validated treatment option. The FDA, a federal agency under the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, is responsible for ensuring the safety, efficacy, and security of human and veterinary drugs, biological products, and medical devices. This approval reflects the agency's ongoing efforts to provide clear guidance on prescription drug apps and software, supporting innovation in the healthcare sector.

FDA's Role and Guidance

This guidance is essential for the development and approval of digital therapeutics.
