FIELD NOTE

The Rise of Data and AI Sovereignty

Data sovereignty has emerged as a defining challenge of the digital age, raising urgent questions about who controls data, who benefits from it, and who is accountable for its use. This is amplified by the strategic importance governments place on AI infrastructure, data, and models, leading to policy discussions around AI sovereignty.

As AI development accelerates, claims for data sovereignty and concerns regarding the extraterritorial legal reach of different jurisdictions are becoming more pronounced. This necessitates examining the legal frameworks available to manage or block foreign access to data.
