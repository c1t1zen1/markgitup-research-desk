FIELD NOTE

The Efficiency Advantage of Apple Silicon for Edge AI

A significant engineering advantage of Apple Silicon for running local LLMs is its power efficiency. One guide notes that Apple Silicon inference consumes between 15 to 30 watts during active LLM generation, contrasting sharply with the 200 to 400 watts required by a high-end NVIDIA GPU under load. This difference translates directly into meaningful electricity savings and reduced thermal output, which is critical for always-on or frequently used local AI applications in home and office settings.

This efficiency is tied to the architecture, which includes a Neural Accelerator within every GPU core. Furthermore, specific chip generations show performance gains; for instance, the M5 Max is noted to decode approximately 12% faster than the M4 Max, attributed to differences in memory bandwidth.
