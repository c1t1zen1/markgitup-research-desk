Closing read

Conclusion

The current evidence strongly indicates that generative AI is maturing alongside the rise of spatial computing. AI is moving into the capture phase (built-in camera editing) and the generation phase (high-fidelity video models). Forecasts suggest that the next major development will be the seamless interaction of these AI tools within immersive, spatial interfaces, allowing users to edit content in 3D space.

However, the sources do not explicitly detail the *workflow* of editing within a spatial interface; they detail the *existence* of the technologies. While the trend is clear, the specific integration points for spatial editing workflows remain largely implied by the parallel advancements in the fields.
