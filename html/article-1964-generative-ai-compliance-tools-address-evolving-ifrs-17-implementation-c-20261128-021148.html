Conclusion

The evidence confirms that GenAI has capabilities relevant to compliance reporting and financial transformation, including automating document generation. However, direct, specific evidence linking GenAI tools to solving *current* IFRS 17 implementation challenges is not present; many sources reference future standards (2026).
