The Regulatory Shift

The SEC, founded to help the country respond to the Great Depression, has historically protected investors from misconduct. Now, the agency faces the challenge of regulating AI-driven trading, which represents a new frontier in market conduct. The SEC can bring civil actions in U.S. District Court or administrative proceedings, providing tools to enforce new rules once they are established.
