FIELD NOTE

The Challenge of Temporal Coherence in AI Video

Current limitations in AI video generation often manifest as an inability to sustain narrative logic over extended periods. One source identifies this issue as the 'Temporal Wall,' noting that maintaining logic over time is exponentially more difficult for a neural network than generating a single static frame. This difficulty necessitates advanced techniques to ensure that generated content remains consistent across many frames. Research in this area views video generation as a sequential sampling process from a high-dimensional spatiotemporal distribution, prompting systematic reviews of the field.
