FIELD NOTE

The Dual Disclosure Mandate

Recent industry analyses suggest that by 2026, the primary compliance standard for AI avatars in commercial video will involve a 'dual disclosure' mechanism. This approach requires marketers to simultaneously label content as an advertisement and as AI-generated. Specifically, sources indicate that the FTC and New York's synthetic performer law will necessitate tags such as #ad for promotional intent and #AIGenerated or #synthetic to denote the creation method. This distinction aims to clarify both the commercial purpose and the synthetic nature of the presenter, ensuring consumers are not misled about the authenticity of the person on screen.

  • Requirement to label promotional nature (e.g., #ad).
  • Requirement to label synthetic creation method (e.g., #AIGenerated).
  • Application to commercial content featuring AI avatars.

Scope of Covered Content

The regulatory scope extends beyond simple avatar animations to include any content where AI has created or significantly altered a depiction of a person. This includes AI-generated images of realistic people, AI-altered photos or videos, and AI-generated voices. The 'Made with AI' label is cited as a current standard for such content as of 2026. This broad definition ensures that virtual presenters, whether they are entirely synthetic or heavily modified real humans, fall under the same disclosure obligations, preventing ambiguity in how 'AI avatar' is defined for legal compliance.

  • Covers AI-generated images of real-looking people.
  • Includes AI-altered photos and videos.
  • Encompasses AI-generated voices of persons.
  • Standard label: 'Made with AI'.
