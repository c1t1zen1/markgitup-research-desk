Closing read

Conclusion

The supplied evidence supports enterprise RAG as a way to connect LLMs with private business knowledge under security and governance requirements. It also identifies relevant concerns around accuracy, privacy, embeddings, authentication, access control, and data isolation. These points make local inference on Mac hardware a plausible application area, but they do not validate a specific Mac deployment.

The projection is that quantized local models could help organizations explore more private or responsive RAG workflows, especially where limiting external data exposure matters. That projection remains unverified here: the sources provide no Mac benchmarks, quantization analysis, latency measurements, or proof that local execution satisfies a company's sovereignty requirements.
