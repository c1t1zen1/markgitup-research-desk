The Regulatory Framework for AI Disclosure

The governance surrounding AI itself is tightening, with specific mandates emerging in 2026. The European Commission finalized guidance on AI transparency requirements in July 2026, including a watermarking mandate for various AI outputs. These regulatory developments indicate a broader push toward transparency in AI systems, which sets a precedent for how AI-generated or AI-analyzed data—such as ESG audits—will be governed.
