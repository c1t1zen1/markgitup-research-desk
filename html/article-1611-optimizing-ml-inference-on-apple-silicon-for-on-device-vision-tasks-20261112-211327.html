FIELD NOTE

The Role and Architecture of the Apple Neural Engine (ANE)

The Apple Neural Engine (ANE) functions as Apple's fixed-function matrix accelerator, designed specifically for ML inference. It has been integrated into every Apple system-on-chip since the A11 in iPhones and iPads, extending to the M1 in Macs. This makes it a widely deployed component in modern Apple devices.

Access to the ANE is typically mediated through Core ML. However, some research has explored methods to reach the engine directly, bypassing the standard Core ML interface, to gain deeper insight into its operation and performance. Documentation regarding the ANE's internal workings is noted as being limited.

  • The ANE is Apple's fixed-function matrix accelerator.
  • It has been integrated into Apple system-on-chips since the A11 (iPhones/iPads) and M1 (Macs).
  • Application interaction with the ANE is generally channeled through Core ML.
