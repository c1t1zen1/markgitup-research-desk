FIELD NOTE

The Shift to Personalized Digital Therapeutics

Digital therapeutics are transitioning from fixed software interventions to adaptive models. This shift allows for dynamic personalized optimization, where AI continuously adjusts treatment based on an individual patient's evolving needs. This level of personalization is crucial because mental health and neurodevelopmental disorders are recognized as highly heterogeneous categories in medicine, making a one-size-fits-all approach insufficient. AI itself is defined as technology that allows computers to simulate human learning and perform tasks commonly associated with humans.
