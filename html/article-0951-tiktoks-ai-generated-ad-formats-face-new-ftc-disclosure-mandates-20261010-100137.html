The FTC’s Enforcement Mandate

The Federal Trade Commission maintains its mission to protect Americans from anticompetitive, unfair, and deceptive business practices. In the context of 2026, this mission has expanded to rigorously address AI-generated content that may mislead consumers. The agency’s focus is on ensuring that consumers are not deceived by synthetic media, particularly in commercial contexts.

Marketers must navigate a 'double disclosure' rule, which involves both platform-specific labeling and federal legal duties. The FTC’s stance is clear: if AI content is used in advertising, it must be clearly disclosed to prevent deception. This federal layer of regulation operates independently of, but concurrently with, platform-specific rules.

TikTok’s Specific AI Labeling Rules

TikTok’s 2026 Community Guidelines have introduced mandatory disclosure for AI-generated content. This includes synthetic faces, voice clones, AI-generated backgrounds, and photorealistic product images. The platform employs a 4-tier label system to categorize and flag this content, ensuring users are aware of its synthetic nature.

For commercial content, TikTok requires the use of cleared audio and specific disclosure settings. The AI-generated label is additionally required when synthetic elements are present. These platform mechanisms are designed to sit alongside, not replace, separate legal duties under FTC and EU rules, creating a layered compliance requirement for advertisers.
