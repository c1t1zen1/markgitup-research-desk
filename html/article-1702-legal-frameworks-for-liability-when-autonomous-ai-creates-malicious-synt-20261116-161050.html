The Liability Vacuum in AI-Generated Harm

The deployment of AI systems that generate content raises fundamental questions about who bears responsibility when the output is flawed or malicious. One observation suggests that certain legal frameworks, such as the one in India, do not yet clearly define liability when AI makes errors in its decision-making processes. This points to a systemic challenge in current legal structures when dealing with autonomous AI outputs.
