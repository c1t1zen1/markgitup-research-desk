FIELD NOTE

The Lawsuit: Velos Media vs. ByteDance

Velos Media LLC filed a patent infringement lawsuit against ByteDance Ltd., claiming that TikTok's video platform uses technology covered by six of its patents. The patents relate to video encoding and decoding technologies that are essential to the H.265/HEVC video compression standard. This standard is widely used in video streaming infrastructure, including TikTok's platform. The lawsuit was filed in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Texas, with docket number 1:25-cv-00967. The case is currently active, according to legal tracking sources.
