Why this matters now

In September 2026, Apple unveiled its A20 Pro system on a chip, marking a significant advancement in on-device AI capabilities. The A20 Pro Chip: A Leap in On-Device AI

Apple's A20 Pro chip represents a major step forward in on-device AI. Built on a 2nm process, the chip is designed to handle more complex AI tasks locally, reducing the need for cloud-based processing. Dedicated Secure Enclave for AI Training

A key feature of the A20 Pro is its dedicated secure enclave for local AI model training. This enclave isolates user data from the main OS, ensuring that even during fine-tuning, the data remains secure. This is a significant advancement in privacy, as it addresses concerns about data exposure during AI processing. The secure enclave is a hardware-based solution, providing a higher level of security compared to software-only approaches.
