FIELD NOTE

Defining Digital Provenance

Provenance is defined as the origin or source of something. More broadly, it encompasses an object's complete documented history. In the context of digital media, establishing a verifiable chain of custody is paramount for legal admissibility. The concept of provenance suggests a need for a comprehensive record detailing how a piece of media came into existence and what transformations it underwent.

  • Provenance refers to an object's origin or source.
  • It covers the complete documented history of an artifact.
