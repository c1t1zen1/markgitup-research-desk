FIELD NOTE

Defining Autonomous Agents in Software Operations

The term 'autonomous' generally describes something that is independent, self-governing, or not subject to external control. In the context of AI, autonomous agents are described as systems that can take actions and complete multi-step goals without constant human input. These agents are capable of functions beyond simple question answering, including browsing the web and executing code.

This shift implies a move from AI as a helper tool to AI as an independent operational entity within the software development lifecycle. The capability for these agents to operate independently is a core characteristic emphasized in the provided material.

Deploying AI agents into production environments requires a specialized approach that goes beyond traditional software CI/CD.
