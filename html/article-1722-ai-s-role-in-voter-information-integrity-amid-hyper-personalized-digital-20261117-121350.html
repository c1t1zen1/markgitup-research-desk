Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence demonstrates a clear technological arms race: as generative AI creates more sophisticated political content, detection and verification technologies are simultaneously advancing. Currently, the landscape is characterized by emerging tracking tools and international regulatory pushes, such as those seen in the EU.

It is important to distinguish between established facts and future projections. While trackers document past incidents, the full enforcement of new regulations or the complete success of detection tools remains subject to future development. The current state suggests a move toward mandatory disclosure and enhanced technical scrutiny.
