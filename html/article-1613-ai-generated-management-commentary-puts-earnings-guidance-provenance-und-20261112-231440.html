FIELD NOTE

The Integration of AI in Earnings Narrative Generation

Generative AI is increasingly utilized by IR departments to assist with earnings-day preparation. This includes refining word choice in prepared remarks and predicting potential questions from analysts during earnings calls. Furthermore, the process of generating the quarterly earnings script—a document that requires integrating financial results, segment performance, management commentary, guidance updates, and strategic framing—can involve AI generating the initial draft by combining multiple inputs.

These AI applications are part of a broader trend where AI in IR is evolving into more complex systems, such as Agentic Systems, which are involved in workflow orchestration and outreach. Modern IR platforms are expected to support these AI-driven processes, which are reshaping how investors engage with disclosures.

  • AI is used to predict and prepare answers for analyst questions on earnings calls.
  • AI assists in refining word choice within prepared remarks.
  • AI can generate the first draft of the quarterly earnings script by synthesizing various inputs.

Conclusion

The evidence clearly demonstrates that AI is a powerful tool accelerating and reshaping the creation of financial narratives, from initial drafting to strategic refinement. Forecasts indicate that AI will continue to drive deeper integration into IR processes, leading to more sophisticated, agentic systems.

However, the sources do not provide insight into the specific governance structures companies are using to manage the risk associated with AI-generated commentary. The transition from AI-assisted drafting to final, accountable disclosure represents a critical juncture where technological capability must meet rigorous legal and ethical oversight. The scrutiny investors are applying is fundamentally about tracing the provenance of the narrative back to a responsible human entity.
