FIELD NOTE

The Evolution of Fraud Detection: From Credentials to Behavior

Traditional security measures are increasingly insufficient against sophisticated threats, as fraudsters can now acquire credentials in bulk. Behavioral biometrics addresses this vulnerability by focusing on the physical interaction patterns of the legitimate customer. This technology analyzes unique aspects of user interaction to build a behavioral baseline for each customer. This shift implies that the ability to fake a physical interaction pattern is the new barrier for fraudsters.

  • Fraudsters often acquire credentials in bulk.
  • Behavioral biometrics analyzes typing cadence, swipe pressure, and device grip angle.
  • The goal is to detect patterns that a fraudster cannot easily replicate.
