Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence strongly indicates that deploying LLMs in HFT is not a simple plug-and-play integration; it constitutes a cutting-edge engineering challenge centered on latency. Current architectural considerations point toward specialized, multi-agent frameworks designed specifically for high-frequency, price-driven tasks, contrasting with the more general text-based reasoning of many LLMs. While LLMs are being explored for generating trade signals in future contexts, the operational reality of HFT demands that any associated overhead, such as tracing, must operate within the same microsecond constraints as the inference itself. Future developments are projected to focus heavily on this optimization and real-time observability.
