FIELD NOTE

The Core Concept: Federated Learning for Privacy

Federated Learning is a mechanism designed to build privacy-preserving systems for machine learning and data science on decentralized data. This approach allows AI model training to occur across multiple decentralized datasets without requiring the raw data to be centralized. The fundamental process involves local model training on edge devices, followed by the aggregation of model updates—rather than the data itself—to a central server. This significantly enhances user privacy by keeping sensitive information local.

  • Federated Learning enables AI model training across decentralized datasets without centralizing raw data.
  • The process involves local model training on edge devices and aggregation of model updates.
