Conclusion

The evidence strongly indicates a paradigm shift in cybersecurity, where AI is moving security operations from a reactive posture to a proactive, hunting-centric model to combat sophisticated nation-state actors. The existence of AI-powered detection platforms and reports detailing AI-orchestrated espionage confirm this operational shift. However, the sources present a dual reality: AI is a powerful defensive tool, yet it is simultaneously being weaponized by adversaries, creating a complex, evolving threat dynamic.
