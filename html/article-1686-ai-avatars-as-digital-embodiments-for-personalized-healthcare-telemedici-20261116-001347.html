For AI avatars to be deployed in clinical consultations, regulatory compliance is paramount, especially when handling Protected Health Information (PHI). Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence indicates that the deployment of AI avatars in telemedicine is moving toward functional, regulated applications rather than purely entertainment use. The critical factor for clinical adoption appears to be the demonstrable capability of the platform to maintain strict compliance, such as HIPAA, when handling Protected Health Information (PHI).

Five takeaways
