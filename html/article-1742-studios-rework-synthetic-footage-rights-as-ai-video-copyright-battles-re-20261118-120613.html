Why synthetic footage could move into contract language

If disputes over generated outputs continue, studios may seek clearer allocation of responsibility for footage created with AI systems. Possible contract topics include who supplies or controls prompts and source materials, who approves generated footage, and who bears clearance or infringement risk. These are forecasts based on the legal questions identified in Source 12, not reported contract practices.
