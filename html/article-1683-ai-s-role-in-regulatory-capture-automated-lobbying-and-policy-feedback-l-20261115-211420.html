Lobbying groups influence proposed laws and regulations through several established methods. Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence clearly demonstrates a massive, quantifiable influx of corporate capital into the AI regulatory sphere, with major players spending billions to shape outcomes. This establishes the *mechanism* of influence. However, the sources do not provide direct evidence of AI systems *automating* the drafting process itself, only that AI is the subject of the lobbying. Therefore, while the risk of systemic regulatory capture is implied by the scale of influence, the specific automation feedback loop remains a projection based on the trend of increasing technological integration in policy.
