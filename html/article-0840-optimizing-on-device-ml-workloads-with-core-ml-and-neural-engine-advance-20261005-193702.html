FIELD NOTE

Hardware Foundations for On-Device Inference

The latest Apple Silicon chips, such as the M6, feature architectural upgrades like the adoption of 2-nanometer processes. This advancement reportedly increases transistor density and power efficiency, allowing for compute block upgrades without increasing the physical die size. The M5 Ultra, for instance, has been noted to show a significant increase in multi-core compute compared to earlier generations like the M1. These hardware leaps in compute capability are foundational for improving the efficiency of running complex ML models locally, which is the core tenet of optimizing workloads via the Neural Engine.

  • The M6 is cited as Apple's first 2-nanometer chip, improving transistor density and power efficiency.
  • The M5 Ultra demonstrates a substantial increase in multi-core compute since the M1.
