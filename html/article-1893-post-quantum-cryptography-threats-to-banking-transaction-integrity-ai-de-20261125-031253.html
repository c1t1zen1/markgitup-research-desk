The Quantum Threat Landscape to Financial Data

Quantum computing poses a significant threat to current encryption methods used in sensitive financial transactions. To counter this, implementing NIST PQC standards, such as CRYSTALS-Kyber, is suggested for quantum-resistant AI security. Furthermore, some frameworks propose using hybrid cryptographic algorithms to protect financial data against future quantum attacks.
