Closing read

Conclusion

The evidence supports a narrower conclusion: Apple Silicon offers an actively documented local-LLM ecosystem with multiple hardware and software choices, and MLX is described as enabling private experimentation, generation, and LoRA fine-tuning. Those capabilities could support privacy-oriented mental-wellness prototypes where keeping inference local is an important design goal.

The stronger claim—that M-Series systems can safely power personalized mental-health assistants—remains a forecast, not a finding. It depends on evidence absent from the supplied material: clinical evaluation, privacy engineering, secure data handling, model behavior testing, crisis safeguards, and appropriate human oversight. Until those signals exist, local execution should be treated as an enabling infrastructure choice rather than proof of therapeutic value or user safety.
