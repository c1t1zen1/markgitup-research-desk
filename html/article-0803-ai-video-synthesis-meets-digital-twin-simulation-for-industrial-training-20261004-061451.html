FIELD NOTE

The Foundation: Digital Twins in Industrial Operations

Digital twins are actively being deployed in industrial manufacturing to virtualize factory operations for simulation, optimization, and product development. These platforms allow for the modeling of factory equipment and production lines. Advanced platforms are incorporating AI/ML-enabled hybrid modeling to advance process simulation and optimization in areas like refining and petrochemicals. Furthermore, the scaling of these digital twins involves combining operational data with physics and AI to improve reliability and reduce costs.

  • Digital twins virtualize factory operations for simulation and optimization.
  • Platforms are advancing process simulation using AI/ML-enabled hybrid modeling.
  • Scaling digital twins requires combining physics, AI, and operational data.
