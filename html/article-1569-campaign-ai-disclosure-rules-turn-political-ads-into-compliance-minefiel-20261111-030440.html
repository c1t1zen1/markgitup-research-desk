Evidence gap should drive the next reporting step

The strongest conclusion supported by the record is not that campaigns already face a defined AI-disclosure minefield. It is that the supplied evidence is insufficient to verify that proposition. The calendar and election-result sources provide timing and context, but no governing rule.

A rigorous follow-up should replace general-event sources with primary legal texts, official election guidance, enforcement records, and documented platform policies. Until that work is done, claims about newly active requirements, operational burdens, or heightened legal risk should be presented as questions or forecasts.
