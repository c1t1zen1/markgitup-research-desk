FIELD NOTE

Automation of LUTs and HDR Workflows

Recent professional guides identify the automation of Look-Up Tables (LUTs) as a primary function of modern AI color grading tools. These systems are designed to handle the complexities of High Dynamic Range (HDR) formats, which have historically been a bottleneck in high-end post-production. By automating the generation and application of LUTs, AI tools aim to streamline the transition from standard dynamic range (SDR) to HDR, a process often requiring manual adjustment.

The evidence points to specialized software solutions that facilitate this automation. For instance, comparisons of video color grading software in 2026 explicitly mention automated SDR-to-HDR enhancement as a key feature. This suggests that the industry is moving toward tools that can intelligently map color spaces rather than relying solely on manual histogram manipulation.
