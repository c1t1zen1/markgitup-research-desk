FDA Clears First AI Therapist as Digital Companion for Chronic Anxiety

The FDA has cleared the first AI therapist as a standalone digital companion for chronic anxiety, marking a regulatory milestone for AI in mental health.

Editor’s brief

Why this matters now

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has granted clearance to the first AI therapist designed as a standalone digital companion for individuals with chronic anxiety. This regulatory approval represents a significant milestone, validating the use of artificial intelligence in mental health treatment and paving the way for broader adoption of AI-driven therapeutic tools. The FDA's clearance process ensures that the product meets established standards for safety and efficacy, providing a foundation for trust in AI-based mental health interventions. This development aligns with the FDA's broader mission to protect public health by ensuring the safety, efficacy, and security of medical products, including digital health innovations.
