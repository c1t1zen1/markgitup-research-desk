FIELD NOTE

Washington State AI Disclosure Landscape

Washington has enacted specific AI disclosure rules. Chapter 42.64 RCW, effective February 1, 2027, requires government agencies making AI systems available to consumers to disclose that the interaction is with an AI system. Furthermore, Washington HB 1170, passed in March 2026, regulates large generative AI providers—those with over $500 million in annual revenue that train AI models and offer services.

  • RCW 42.64 mandates AI system disclosure to consumers by government agencies (Effective Feb 1, 2027).
  • HB 1170 regulates large generative AI providers in Washington (Passed March 2026).
