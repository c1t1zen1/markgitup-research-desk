FIELD NOTE

Operational Testing of Swarm Capabilities

The U.S. military is actively stress-testing drone swarm technology. The U.S. Army conducted exercises involving swarms of up to 100 drones, alongside a mothership, to challenge existing attack helicopters and sensors at Fort Bragg. Another test involved the XVIII Airborne Corps evaluating artificial intelligence, electronic warfare, radar, and autonomous systems against mass drone attacks during Scarlet Dragon 26-3.

These exercises suggest a focus on testing the resilience and overwhelming potential of coordinated drone formations. The U.S. Air Force is also reportedly seeking rapidly deployable drone swarms for base security, indicating a broad military interest in this capability.
