FIELD NOTE

Defining Artificial Intelligence and AI Capabilities

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is broadly defined as technology that allows machines to simulate human learning, problem-solving, and task performance. Various sources describe AI as enabling computers to learn from experience, adjust to new inputs, and perform human-like tasks. It is a technology that processes information, learns from patterns, and predicts outcomes to assist with tasks. Some sources specify that AI encompasses fields of computer and data science focused on building machines with human intelligence.

  • AI enables machines to perform tasks commonly associated with humans.
  • AI allows machines to learn from experience and adjust to new inputs.
  • AI is a set of technologies empowering computers to learn, reason, and perform advanced tasks.

Conclusion
